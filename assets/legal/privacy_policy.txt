This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the requirements of the Apple App Store, Google Play, EU GDPR / UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, the EU AI Act transparency obligations, and other applicable data protection laws. 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how PHA Personal Health Assistant. Developed by Titan Capital Trade LLC ("we", "us", "our", the "Company"), the developer and operator of the PHA Personal Health Assistant mobile application ("App", "Service"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use the App. The App is a personal wellness tracking and AI-powered suggestion tool that helps users input health and lifestyle data and receive personalized general wellness recommendations generated by artificial intelligence.

By using the App, you consent to the collection and processing of your information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information You Provide Directly
• Account and Profile Data: Name, email address, age range, gender (optional), country/region, password (hashed).
• Health and Wellness Data (Special Category / Sensitive Data): Data you voluntarily input such as weight, height, blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose, sleep patterns, physical activity logs, symptoms, goals, dietary preferences, medication/supplement logs, mood, and other wellness-related information you choose to enter.
• Connected Health Data (with your explicit permission): If you choose to connect Apple HealthKit, Google Health Connect, or similar services, we may read and/or write specific data categories (e.g., steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, blood pressure) to provide better personalization. You control permissions via your device settings.
• AI Interaction Data: The questions, inputs, and context you provide to the AI assistant, and the responses generated, to maintain conversation history and improve personalization within your account.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically
• Device and Technical Data: Device type, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers (IDFA/GAID only if you opt-in), IP address, language, time zone, crash logs and diagnostic data.
• Usage and Analytics Data: How you interact with the App (screens viewed, features used, session duration, AI recommendation acceptance rates) to improve the Service. We use aggregated analytics where possible.

2.3 Information from Third Parties
We may receive limited information from Apple, Google, or other sign-in providers if you use "Sign in with Apple" or Google Sign-In. We do not receive your password from these providers.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect for the following purposes (legal bases indicated where relevant under GDPR):
• Provide and Personalize the Service (Contract + Consent): Generate AI-powered wellness recommendations, insights, and suggestions tailored to the data you provide and (with permission) data from connected health platforms.
• Improve AI Models and Service Quality (Legitimate Interest + Consent for health data): Train and refine our AI systems using aggregated, anonymized, or pseudonymized data. Health data used for model improvement is minimized and protected.
• Account Management and Security (Contract + Legitimate Interest): Create and secure your account, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to your requests and support inquiries.
• Analytics and Product Development (Legitimate Interest): Understand usage patterns, fix bugs, and develop new features.
• Legal Compliance and Safety (Legal Obligation): Comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests, enforce our Terms of Service.
• Communications: Send important service updates, security alerts, and (only with your consent) occasional newsletters or feature announcements (you can opt out anytime).

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA/UK: We process personal data on the following legal bases under the GDPR:
• Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)): For processing of special-category health data and for AI-powered personalization features. You can withdraw consent at any time via App settings or by contacting us.
• Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): To deliver the Service you requested (account creation, AI recommendations based on your inputs).
• Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To improve the App, ensure security, prevent fraud, and conduct aggregated analytics (balanced against your rights).
• Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): To comply with laws and respond to valid legal requests.

5. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:
• AI and Cloud Service Providers (Data Processors): We use reputable third-party AI model providers (e.g., xAI / Grok API, and potentially specialized health AI services such as NVIDIA Clara or similar) and cloud hosting providers. These providers process data only on our instructions under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) that include confidentiality, security, and (for EU data) Standard Contractual Clauses. They do not use your data to train their own models unless you explicitly opt in to anonymized improvement programs.
• Analytics and Infrastructure Providers: Aggregated or pseudonymized usage data may be shared with analytics tools (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar) to help us understand how the App is used.
• Legal and Safety: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the Company, or the public.
• Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.
• Aggregated / Anonymized Data: We may share fully aggregated and anonymized statistics that cannot be used to identify you for research, marketing, or improvement purposes.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements:
• Account and Health Data: Retained while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time via App settings or by contacting us. After deletion, data is removed from active systems within 30 days (backups may persist up to 90 days for disaster recovery).
• AI Conversation History: Retained for the duration of your active use to maintain context; you can clear individual conversations or request full deletion.
• Analytics / Usage Data: Aggregated or pseudonymized data may be retained longer for product improvement.
• Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law (e.g., tax, accounting, or fraud prevention records).
